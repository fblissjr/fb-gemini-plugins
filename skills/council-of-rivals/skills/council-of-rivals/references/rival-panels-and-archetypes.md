# Rival Panels & Orthogonal Casts (`rival-panels-and-archetypes.md`)

12 ready-to-convene **3-Seat and 4-Seat Rival Panels** across everyday
non-coding, intellectual, creative, personal, and technical domains. Each panel
is engineered so the seats have **structurally incompatible objective functions**
that force genuine collision and expose the exact **Double Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**.

---

## 1. Career & Life Crossroads Panel (*"Should I take this leap?"*)
- **Seat 1 — The Asymmetric Slope Maximizer**: Optimizes for learning velocity,
  optionality, and high-variance upside while fixed obligations are manageable.
- **Seat 2 — The Ergodicity & Floor Guardian *(Taleb-style)***: Optimizes for
  zero ruin, psychological safety, cashflow continuity, and protecting non-work
  life.
- **Seat 3 — Future-You at Age 75 *(Regret Minimization + Tuesday Reality)***:
  Cares zero about resume prestige; cares entirely about daily craft enjoyment
  at 10:30 AM on a random Tuesday and avoiding permanent regret.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether the downside of the leap is
  *reversible within 12 months* (if reversible, Seat 2's ruin objection
  collapses; if irreversible, Seat 1's upside bet is too fragile).

---

## 2. Writing, Essay & Keynote Critique Panel (*"Why isn't this draft landing?"*)
- **Seat 1 — The Impatient Commuter (Reader with 20% Attention)**: Skims on a
  phone; ruthlessly flags the exact sentence where they get bored and close the
  tab.
- **Seat 2 — The Hostile Fact-Checker / Skeptic**: Hunts for unbacked claims,
  weasel words, and logical leaps; demands concrete mechanisms or numbers.
- **Seat 3 — The Literary Stylist *(Carver / Orwell Lens)***: Cuts 30% of word
  count, deletes throat-clearing intros, and replaces abstract adjectives with
  concrete sensory nouns and verbs.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether the real thesis is buried in
  Paragraph 4 instead of Sentence 1.

---

## 3. Product, Pricing & Go-To-Market Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Churned / Skeptical Buyer**: Doesn't care about your roadmap;
  only cares about switching cost, time-to-first-value, and why their current
  spreadsheet/incumbent isn't good enough.
- **Seat 2 — The Ruthless Short-Seller / Competitor PM**: Explains exactly how
  an incumbent or fast-follower will clone or undercut the feature in 60 days.
- **Seat 3 — The High-Conviction Growth Lead**: Focuses on wedge distribution,
  pricing power, and the 10x hook for early adopters.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether customers experience the pain
  *frequently and acutely enough* to change an existing habit without hand-held
  onboarding.

---

## 4. Personal Finance, Capital Allocation & Investing Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Quality Compounder *(Munger / Buffett Lens)***: Demands
  durable moats, low churn, simple unit economics, and a 10-year holding horizon.
- **Seat 2 — The Macro / Tail-Risk Skeptic**: Stress-tests liquidity crunches,
  correlated drawdowns, hidden leverage, and regime shifts.
- **Seat 3 — The Behavioral Economist *(Kahneman / Thaler Lens)***: Audits how
  *your own psychology* will actually behave during a 40% drawdown or cashflow
  squeeze.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether your liquidity buffer allows you to
  hold through a 24-month worst-case winter without forced selling.

---

## 5. Interpersonal, Family & Relationship Boundary Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Radical Candor / Self-Respect Advocate**: Refuses to let you
  swallow resentment, over-apologize, or subsidize bad behavior.
- **Seat 2 — The Systems / Attachment Therapist**: Looks past the surface
  argument to the other person's unspoken fear, exhaustion, or threat response.
- **Seat 3 — The Pragmatic Peacekeeper / Game Theorist**: Evaluates what happens
  on Day 2 *after* the conversation—Focusing on sustainable household/team norms
  rather than "winning" the argument.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether the conflict is a **bad-faith
  boundary violation** (Seat 1's firm line wins) or a **broken environment/system
  between two exhausted allies** (Seat 2 & 3's collaborative redesign wins).

---

## 6. Habit, Health & Personal Operating System Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Peak-Performance Coach**: Pushes for progressive overload,
  high standards, and measurable biological/output gains.
- **Seat 2 — The "Tired Tuesday at 7 PM" Realist**: Assumes you will have 20%
  willpower, a late meeting, and poor sleep twice a week; attacks any plan that
  requires heroic daily discipline.
- **Seat 3 — The Environment Architect**: Ignores motivation entirely; only
  cares about physical friction (what is within arm's reach vs. locked away).
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether the habit survives a 3-day disruption
  via a Minimum Viable Habit (MVH) floor.

---

## 7. Policy, Economics & Institutional Design Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Public-Interest Reformer**: Optimizes for equity, access, and
  solving the visible first-order problem.
- **Seat 2 — The Public-Choice / Goodhart Cynic**: Assumes every actor (agencies,
  corporations, citizens) will immediately game the metric or exploit the
  regulatory loophole.
- **Seat 3 — The Second-Order Supply/Incentive Economist**: Traces how price
  caps, mandates, or subsidies shift long-term supply and equilibrium behavior.
- **Typical Crux ($\mathcal{C}$)**: Whether the enforcement/metric mechanism can
  be gamed cheaper than complying with the spirit of the rule.

---

## 8. Scientific Research & Hypothesis Stress-Test Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Bold Theorist**: Defends the novel mechanism and its explanatory
  reach.
- **Seat 2 — The Methodological Assassin *(Reviewer #2 / Feynman Lens)***: Hunts
  for confounders, selection bias, p-hacking, survivorship bias, and mundane
  alternative explanations.
- **Seat 3 — The Experimental Pragmatist**: Designs the cheapest 48-hour
  falsification test that separates the novel hypothesis from the null.

---

## 9. Legal, Contract & Negotiation Strategy Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Litigator (Worst-Case Dispute Lens)**: Reads every clause
  assuming the relationship has already blown up and you are in court.
- **Seat 2 — The Commercial Deal-Maker**: Refuses to let 1% edge-case paranoia
  kill a high-value partnership or stall closing by 6 weeks.
- **Seat 3 — The Operational Owner**: Asks who actually has to execute the SLA
  or compliance covenant on Monday morning.

---

## 10. Creative Worldbuilding, Fiction & Screenwriting Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Structural Showrunner**: Demands relentless scene tension,
  clear character desire/obstacle in every beat, and zero filler scenes.
- **Seat 2 — The Internal-Logic / Lore Auditor**: Checks whether character
  choices and world rules make sense given their incentives (no "idiot plots").
- **Seat 3 — The Emotional Resonance / Subtext Critic**: Ensures the emotional
  payoff is earned through action rather than exposition.

---

## 11. Philosophy, Ethics & Mental Model Deconstruction Panel
- **Seat 1 — The Consequentialist / Expected-Value Optimizer**
- **Seat 2 — The Deontologist / Sacred-Constraint Defender**
- **Seat 3 — The Virtue / Character & Cultural Evolution Thinker**

---

## 12. Software Architecture & Engineering Trade-Off Panel
- **Seat 1 — The "Boring Technology" Monolith Pragmatist**: Optimizes for
  shipping today, zero operational complexity, and 1-command debugging.
- **Seat 2 — The Scale & Isolation Architect**: Optimizes for fault boundaries,
  contract strictness, and 10x load readiness.
- **Seat 3 — The 3:00 AM On-Call Engineer**: Judges every design by how
  understandable and recoverable it is when paging alerts fire at 3:00 AM.
