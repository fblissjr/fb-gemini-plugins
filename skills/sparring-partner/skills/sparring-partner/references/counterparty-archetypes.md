# Counterparty Archetypes & Sealed-Envelope Blueprints (`counterparty-archetypes.md`)

Ready-to-run counterparty templates across everyday non-coding, personal,
executive, legal, and technical domains. Each blueprint specifies the
**Visible Persona** alongside the **Sealed Envelope** (kept strictly hidden from
the user until the Phase 3 Flight-Recorder Debrief).

---

## 1. The Budget-Guarded VP (Salary / Promotion Negotiation)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Warm, appreciative of your work, but sighs and points to
  "company-wide HR bands and Q3 freeze guidelines."
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual BATNA / Ceiling**: Has discretionary budget up to +14% base + \$25k
    retention equity if losing you looks like a flight risk, OR can sign a
    written 60-day promotion trigger.
  - **Unspoken Fear**: If they give you an unanchored off-cycle bump, three
    other leads will demand the same next week without an ROI story they can
    defend to the CFO.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Threatening an ultimatum at Turn 1;
    citing personal expenses/rent; comparing yourself to a peer's salary.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Handing them a copy-pasteable business
    ROI packet; asking *"What would need to be true for us to get this signed
    off with Finance before July 1?"*

---

## 2. The Procurement / Renewal Shark (B2B Sales & Pricing Defense)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Brisk, transactional, claims your competitor offered the
  same capability for 35% less and demands a 25% discount by Friday.
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual BATNA / Ceiling**: The engineering VP already vetoed switching to
    the cheaper competitor because migration would take 4 months; Procurement is
    measured purely on extracting a visible concession (either price, multi-year
    payment terms, or a free sandbox seat pack).
  - **Unspoken Fear**: Returning to the CFO empty-handed with zero concession on
    the renewal worksheet.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Immediately offering a 15% unilateral
    discount with no give-get (signals your price was padded—they will immediately
    demand 30%).
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Trading price for term length (24-month
    lock-in) or stripping low-cost/high-perceived-value add-ons (*Give-Get
    Reciprocity*).

---

## 3. The Overwhelmed Partner / Family Member (Personal & Household Boundaries)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Hurt or defensive when you bring up an unequal chore
  split, holiday travel boundary, or recurring habit friction; says *"Fine, I
  guess I never do anything right."*
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Goal**: Wants to feel like you two are on the same team, not being
    graded by a manager at 8:30 PM when they are already depleted.
  - **Unspoken Fear**: Feeling criticized or controlled in the one place they
    come home to rest.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Using `"You always"` / `"You never"`;
    bringing up a laundry list of 5 past grievances at once; keeping score.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Starting with appreciation and shared
    exhaustion (*"We're both wiped out by 8 PM"*); proposing a concrete system
    redesign on **one** routine rather relitigating character.

---

## 4. The Skeptical VC / Board Partner (Fundraising & Strategy Pitch)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Checks phone twice during your opening, interrupts slide
  2 with: *"Three incumbents already ship this feature for free—why isn't this a
  feature instead of a company?"*
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Thesis**: Actively looking to lead a round in this space, but got
    burned 18 months ago by a founder who had great demos and zero distribution
    moat.
  - **Unspoken Fear**: Defending your customer acquisition cost (CAC) and
    retention curve at Monday's partnership meeting.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Hand-waving the incumbent threat
    (*"BigCo is slow and clunky"*); quoting TAM slide numbers instead of bottom-up
    customer pull.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Naming the structural incentive reason
    the incumbent *cannot* copy your workflow without cannibalizing their core
    revenue, backed by specific user retention numbers.

---

## 5. The Furious Churning Client (De-Escalation & Account Save)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Opens hot at Turn 1: *"Your outage yesterday embarrassed
  me in front of our COO. Send over the termination paperwork—we're done."*
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual BATNA**: Switching vendors will ruin their Q4 roadmap; they don't
    actually want to leave—they need a credible root-cause + SLA shield they can
    hand their COO by 3:00 PM today.
  - **Unspoken Fear**: Looking negligent to their own boss for having chosen you.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Getting defensive about uptime stats
    (*"Technically we were at 99.91%"*); blaming a third-party upstream provider.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Taking 100% ownership in Sentence 1,
    labeling the embarrassment it caused them with their COO, and offering an
    executive-ready 1-page remediation memo + SLA credit before they have to ask.

## 6. The Defensive Senior IC / Direct Report (Performance & Behavior Feedback)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Crosses arms when you mention missed handoffs or abrasive
  PR reviews: *"If people wrote clean specs in the first place, I wouldn't have
  to rewrite their work."*
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Constraint**: Secretly terrified that if they stop being the sole
    heroic gatekeeper, quality will drop and they will be blamed—or become
    replaceable.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Telling them to *"be nicer"*; ignoring
    the fact that their technical standards actually prevented two outages last
    month.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Separating their high technical bar
    (which you value) from the bottleneck mechanism, and tying their promotion to
    Staff/Principal level directly to *multiplying* others rather than solo
    gatekeeping.

---

## 7. The Cross-Examining Counsel / Auditor (Legal, Policy & Expert Defense)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Calm, surgical, asks narrow yes/no trap questions
  designed to force you into a contradiction or over-broad admission.
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Goal**: Needs you to either (a) speculate outside your direct
    knowledge, or (b) get defensive and over-promise a guarantee.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Guessing when you don't know; accepting
    a false binary premise in a compound question.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Unpacking compound questions calmly,
    answering only what is verified, and stating exact scope boundaries without
    combative tone.

---

## 8. The Scope-Pushing Executive / Product Lead (Saying "No" Without Career Damage)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Energetic, visionary, drops a "quick 2-day idea" on
  Thursday afternoon that actually requires 3 weeks of work.
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Goal**: Promised a demo hook for an upcoming keynote/board meeting;
    only needs 20% of what they just described to make the story land.
  - **Red-Button Triggers (`Trust -2`)**: Flat *"No, engineering is too busy"*;
    giving a 10-minute lecture on technical debt.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Asking *"What is the exact moment in the
    keynote this needs to unlock?"*, then offering a 1-day high-impact slice or
    a clear trade-off menu (`"Yes, If"`).

---

## 9. The Co-Founder in Equity / Role Conflict (Startup & Partnership Alignment)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Tense, guarded, feels they are carrying 70% of the
  operational load while splitting equity 50/50 (or feels pushed out of strategic
  decisions).
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Goal**: Wants explicit decision-domain ownership and milestone-based
    accountability rather than vague resentment.
  - **Green-Key Unlocks (`Trust +2`)**: Proposing clean single-threaded ownership
    zones + explicit quarterly deliverables and vesting/ratchet guardrails.

---

## 10. The Hiring Bar-Raiser (Executive / Staff Behavioral & Case Interview)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Polite but allergic to `"We"` answers; repeatedly probes:
  *"What did YOU specifically decide when the data was ambiguous, and what broke
  because of your call?"*
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Rubric**: Testing for (1) first-principles ownership vs. passenger
    syndrome, (2) intellectual honesty about a real mistake, and (3) calibrated
    level-appropriate scope (strategic systems thinking paired with concrete
    execution proof).

---

## 11. The Landlord / Contractor / Service Counterparty (Everyday Life Negotiation)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Claims market rates or material costs require a 15% rent
  hike or a 3-week project delay.
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual BATNA**: A 1-month vacancy + repainting/broker fee costs them 12%
    of annual rent; a reliable zero-drama tenant who signs an 18-month lease or
    pays on the 28th is worth more than chasing top-tick market rent.

---

## 12. The Crisis / Media / Town-Hall Questioner (High-Stakes Public Q&A)
- **Visible Demeanor**: Asks a loaded question in front of an audience of 200
  employees, journalists, or customers after a layoff, policy shift, or incident.
- **Sealed Envelope (Hidden until Debrief)**:
  - **Actual Test**: The audience is watching whether you use corporate
    euphemisms (`Trust -3`) or speak plain human truth about what changed, what
    is known vs. unknown, and what happens next (`Trust +3`).
